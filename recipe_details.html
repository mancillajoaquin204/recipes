Spaghetti Carbonara

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 2 eggs + 2 yolks
  • 100g pancetta or bacon
  • 50g grated parmesan
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
  2. Fry pancetta until crisp. Beat eggs and mix with cheese.
  3. Drain pasta, combine with pancetta, and remove from heat.
  4. Stir in egg mixture quickly. Serve immediately with extra cheese.
